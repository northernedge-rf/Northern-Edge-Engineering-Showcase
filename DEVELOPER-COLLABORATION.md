# Developer-hardware collaboration

Northern Edge Robotics & Fabrication is interested in **developer hardware evaluation, engineering collaboration and technical feedback partnerships**.

## Proposed evaluation

We would work with a hardware partner to agree on a suitable non-confidential workload, repeat relevant tests across available development machines, and produce a concise report about execution, consistency and performance.

Our current evaluation context includes a GTX 1660 Ti laptop and rented RTX 5090 cloud GPU time. Access to more capable local development hardware would help expand testing across our standalone graphics and robotics software projects.

A hardware loan, developer sponsorship or co-marketing arrangement would be discussed **only if the provider is interested**. We make no claim of an existing partnership or endorsement.

For technical introductions or a proposed evaluation brief, contact **northernedge.rf@gmail.com**.
